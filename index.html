About The Initiative

Behind Every Screen, A Human Story

Mindscape is a mental health awareness initiative addressing mobile phone addiction, doomscrolling, and digital fatigue among students across Chennai and beyond.

THEME 01

Behind The Screen

Explore the silent isolation of algorithmic feeds, social media comparison, and how constant connectivity can detach us from genuine human empathy.

THEME 02

Look Up From The Feed

Reclaiming real-world connection, presence, sleep hygiene, and mindfulness. Highlighting what is rediscovered when the screen turns off.

THEME 03

Creative Storytelling

Tell your story through drama, satire, humor, visual poetry, animation, or short sketches. Deliver an impactful message that sparks genuine self-reflection.

Duration 25 to 45 Seconds

Official Competition Rulebook

Rules & Regulations

To ensure fairness, creative authenticity, safety, and responsible mental health advocacy, all participating teams must adhere strictly to the guidelines detailed below.

ELIGIBILITY

A. Eligibility

  • Open to individuals aged 18 years and above.
  • Participants from any educational background are welcome.
  • Entries must be submitted as a team.
  • Participation certificates will be provided to all eligible participants.
CONTENT STANDARDS

B. Content Guidelines

The reel must:
  • Promote mental health awareness and responsible awareness of mobile phone addiction.
  • Be suitable for public viewing.
  • Avoid vulgar, obscene, abusive, offensive, or adult content (18+).
  • Avoid disrespectful acts, gestures, language, or behaviour.
  • Not promote bullying, harassment, discrimination, hatred, or humiliation.
  • Not glorify or encourage harmful behaviour.
CREATIVE INTEGRITY

C. Originality

  • The reel must be original content created by the participating team.
  • Plagiarism or copying another creator's work is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants are responsible for obtaining permission for copyrighted music, footage, images, and other third-party materials.
  • AI tools may be used for editing, animation, voice-over, or design. However, the concept, script, and presentation must reflect the team's originality.
THEMATIC FOCUS

D. Relevance to the Theme

  • The reel must clearly communicate a message related to mobile phone addiction.
  • Creative interpretation is encouraged.
  • Content unrelated to the theme will not be considered.
  • The competition aims to promote awareness, empathy, education, emotional well-being, early support, help-seeking, recovery, and hope.
  • Avoid sensationalism, shock value, tragedy, and harmful portrayals of mental health.
SUBMISSION SPECS

E. Submission Guidelines

  • The completed reel must be submitted on or before 8 November 2026.
  • Use the specified website submission method.
  • Participants must ensure that the submitted video is clear and of good quality.
  • Once submitted, changes may not be permitted unless allowed by the organisers.

F. Legal & Ethical Guidelines

Principles governing ethical storytelling, sensitive advocacy, participant consent, and public safety.

LEGAL & ETHICS
01 Content & Theme
  • Treat mental health topics with sensitivity, empathy, accuracy, and responsibility.
  • Mental health experiences must be presented respectfully.
  • Do not mock, shame, ridicule, or stereotype people experiencing mental health difficulties.
  • Avoid spreading myths, misinformation, or misleading claims.
02 Respect & Privacy
  • Obtain consent from all identifiable individuals appearing in the reel.
  • Obtain permission before recording anyone.
  • Do not record or use personal information without permission.
  • Parental or guardian consent is required if minors appear.
03 Accuracy & Responsibility
  • Ensure all information presented is accurate and responsible.
  • Promote awareness, empathy, understanding, and positive mental well-being.
  • Avoid misleading claims about mental health or mobile phone addiction.
04 Copyright & AI Usage
  • Participants are responsible for ensuring that music, images, videos, graphics, and other materials do not violate copyright regulations.
  • Obtain the necessary permissions for third-party content.
  • AI tools are permitted for editing, animation, voice-over, and design, but the creative concept and presentation must remain original.
05 Safety
  • Filming must not involve dangerous activities, restricted areas, public disturbances, or situations that compromise the safety or privacy of others.
06 Final Decision
  • Any submission violating the rules, regulations, or ethical guidelines may be disqualified.
  • The judging panel's decision regarding eligibility and winner selection shall be final and binding.

H. Disqualification

An entry may be disqualified if:
IMPORTANT NOTE
  • It violates any of the rules mentioned in the rulebook.
  • It contains 18+ or adult content.
  • It contains vulgar, obscene, abusive, or offensive content.
  • It includes disrespectful acts, language, or behaviour.
  • It is unrelated to the given theme.
  • It contains plagiarised or unauthorised content.
  • False or misleading registration information is provided.
  • The reel is submitted after the specified deadline.
